---
title: "Luma Range"
description: "Keeps a brightness band and makes the rest transparent."
---

Keeps areas with brightness between `Min` and `Max` and makes the rest
transparent. The original colors remain unchanged.

| Parameter | Control | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Min` | Trough | 0 to 1 | Lower brightness boundary. |
| `Max` | Trough | 0 to 1 | Upper brightness boundary. |
| `Softness` | Trough | 0 to 0.5 | Softens both boundaries. |
