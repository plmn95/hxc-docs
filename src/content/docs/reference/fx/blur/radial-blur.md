---
title: "Radial Blur"
description: "Streaks the image outward from a center point, producing a zoom blur."
---

Streaks the image outward from a center point, producing a zoom blur.

| Parameter | Control | Range |
|---|---|---|
| `Center X` / `Center Y` | Pad | -0.5 to 0.5 |
| `Strength` | Trough | 0 to 1 |
