Increases or decreases contrast around mid-gray.

| Parameter | Control | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Amount` | Trough | -1 to 2 | -1 makes the image solid mid-gray. 0 preserves the original contrast. Positive values increase contrast, clipping values outside black and white. |
