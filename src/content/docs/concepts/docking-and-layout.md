---
title: "Docking and Layout"
description: "Arrange Hex Composer windows by docking, resizing, tabbing, and using the View menu."
---

The various aspects of Hex Composer's interface are separated into `windows`, which can be shown, hidden, and arranged individually into dockable panes and tabs within them. A given arrangement of `windows` can be saved and restored as a `layout`.

The visible/hidden `windows` and any saved `layouts` are listed in the `View` menu.

Windows can be moved by clicking and dragging their titles. While a window is being dragged, docking zones will appear at the edges of the screen and at the center of the pane where the cursor is currently located. Dropping a window on the zones at the screen edges will dock it along the full height or width of the screen. Dropping a window on the zones at the center of a pane will dock it relative to that pane.

The centermost zone will add the window as a separate tab which uses the entire pane when selected. The four surrounding zones divide the pane vertically or horizontally and place the window on the corresponding side of the split. Some complex relationships of three or more panes may require certain panes to be docked before or after other panes, sequentially.
