- [Reading the Panel](reading-the-panel/): identify Knobs, Troughs, Wheels,

- [AUX Sends and Buses](aux-sends-and-buses/): send a module's image to a
  bus and read it back with `INSERT` or `FEEDBACK`.
- [Chain Groups](chain-groups/): keep related modules together in `Chain`.
- [Mixer Mode](mixer-mode/): the two-deck live-performance mode.
- [Output and Recording](output-and-recording/): record a live session or

- [Export Video](export-video/): render at an exact frame rate, make a
  repeating loop, or export with a song.
- [Docking and Layout](docking-and-layout/): the dockable window system and
