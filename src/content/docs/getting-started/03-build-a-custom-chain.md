---
title: "Make a Kaleidoscope"
description: "Build a six-segment kaleidoscope from the default OSC module and its Insert FX chain."
---

Choose `File > Reset Active Patch to Default` to start with one `OSC`
module in the chain.

## Shape the pattern

Select `OSC` in `Chain`. Under `Waveform` in `Module`, choose the
sine-wave icon.

Expand `FREQUENCY`. Enter `3` in `Freq X` and `2` in `Freq Y`, pressing `Enter` after each
value. Leave the other controls at their defaults.

![OSC frequency controls with Freq X set to 3 and Freq Y set to 2.](../../images/getting-started/frequency.png)

## Add the kaleidoscope

Expand `INSERT FX`, click `+ Add`, and choose `Kaleidoscope` from the
effect type Selector.

Click the numeric field for `Segments`, enter `6`, and press `Enter`.
Leave `Rotation` at `0`; the next page animates it.

![Kaleidoscope in OSC’s Insert FX chain, with Segments set to 6 and Rotation at 0.](../../images/getting-started/kaleidoscope-controls.png)

The effect runs inside `OSC`'s Insert FX chain.
