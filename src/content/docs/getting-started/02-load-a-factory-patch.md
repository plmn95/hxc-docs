---
title: "Load a Factory Patch"
description: "Load a factory patch and save a copy."
---

Open `File > Factory Patches` and choose "Big Brother".

![The File menu's Factory Patches list, including Big Brother.](../../images/getting-started/factory-menu.png)

Select `SPRITE` in `Chain`. In `Module`, set `Opacity` to `0.90` and
press `Enter`.

Choose `File > Save As...` or press `Ctrl+Shift+S` (`Cmd+Shift+S` on
macOS). Save your copy as `First factory patch.hxc` in a folder you can
find again.

Use `File > Open...` to reopen your saved copy. Check that `SPRITE`
`Opacity` is still `0.90` before moving on.
