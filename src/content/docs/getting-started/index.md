---

Make a moving kaleidoscope and record a short clip. First, get familiar
with the workspace and save a factory patch. Then build one pattern,
animate its rotation, and record it.

![The finished six-segment kaleidoscope, with colored mirrored bands.](../images/getting-started/kaleidoscope-result.png)

1. [First Launch](01-first-launch/): find the panels and restore the default layout.
2. [Load a Factory Patch](02-load-a-factory-patch/): explore a patch and save your copy.
3. [Make a Kaleidoscope](03-build-a-custom-chain/): turn the default oscillator into a kaleidoscope.
4. [Animate Your Pattern](05-animate-your-pattern/): connect an LFO to its rotation.
5. [Record and Output](04-record-and-output/): save the patch and record ten seconds.

[Download the finished kaleidoscope](../examples/first-kaleidoscope.hxc)
if you want to compare your patch.
