Hex Composer is a video synthesizer you play.

## Start here

[Make and record your first moving pattern](getting-started/): load a factory
patch, build a kaleidoscope, animate it with an LFO, and record a short clip.

## Choose a task

- [Make an audio spectrum](guides/audio-spectrum/): play a song through a
  ring that expands with its frequencies.
- [Control a parameter with MIDI](concepts/midi-and-opensoundcontrol-learn/):
  connect a controller and learn an assignment.
- [Create feedback trails](guides/feedback-trails/): make a moving ring leave
  fading copies behind it, starting from a supplied patch.
- [Record or send live output](concepts/output-and-recording/): save
  a clip or send the output to another application.
- [Troubleshoot a problem](troubleshooting/): check recording, controller
  input, and external output by symptom.
- [Get help and shared patches](https://discord.gg/Mfx6Jkffkj): ask in the
  Hex Composer Discord's help channel or download patches from its forum. In
  the app, use `Help > Join the Discord...`.

## Understand the system

[Concepts](concepts/) explains chain order, modulation, patch banks,
AUX routing, and mixing.

[Reference](reference/) lists module and effect controls, modulation
