- [Make an Audio Spectrum](audio-spectrum/): play a song through a ring of points.
- [Leave a Trail with Feedback](feedback-trails/): add fading trails to a pulsing ring.

The audio spectrum and feedback recipes include starting patches. For
building and recording your first pattern from scratch, start with
[Getting Started](../getting-started/).
