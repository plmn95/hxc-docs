description: "Recipes for audio-reactive motion, feedback effects and live beat synchronization."

- [Make an Audio Spectrum](audio-spectrum/): play a song through a ring of points.
- [Leave a Trail with Feedback](feedback-trails/): add fading trails to a pulsing ring.
- [Make a CRT Feedback Tunnel](crt-feedback/): shape recursive hexagons with decay and CRT glow.
- [Sync Visuals with Ableton Link](ableton-link/): follow another app's tempo and beat phase.

The audio spectrum and feedback recipes include patches. For building and
recording your first pattern from scratch, start with
[Getting Started](../getting-started/).
